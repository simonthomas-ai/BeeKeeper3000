# BeeKeeper3000 community outreach kit

## One-sentence introduction

BeeKeeper3000 is an open, local-first toolkit that helps beekeepers and regional groups test privacy-preserving insect observations, optional station telemetry and carefully validated search hypotheses for the Asian hornet (*Vespa velutina*).

## English invitation

**Subject:** Invitation to discuss a privacy-first BeeKeeper3000 pilot for Asian hornet monitoring

Hello,

We are developing BeeKeeper3000 as an open, locally operated toolkit for beekeepers, regional observation groups and research partners. The aim is to connect insect observations, human review and— as a separate experiment—station-bearing telemetry, while keeping raw media and precise private locations local by default.

We are looking for a small number of apiaries and experienced partners for a bounded pilot. The pilot will evaluate installation effort, data quality, false positives, radio links and practical usefulness. The project does not replace official reporting channels and does not independently confirm species or nest locations.

Would a short technical exchange, or sharing this invitation with interested members, be appropriate? We would be glad to explain the architecture, privacy controls and current technical limitations.

## Public repository issue template guidance

The public interest form should collect only:
- Country and broad region (not an exact apiary location).
- Participation type (beekeeper, association, expert reviewer, researcher, developer, interested citizen).
- Optional station capabilities (camera, local compute, LoRa/MeshCore, none yet).
- Preferred language and a short non-sensitive message.

Do not collect private email addresses, phone numbers, exact GPS, photos, access credentials or home addresses in a public issue. A public issue is visible to everyone. Offer private contact only through a separately configured, consent-based channel.

## Outreach operating rules

- Human review before every first contact and follow-up.
- One tailored first message per public organisational contact route.
- One follow-up after 14 days; then suppress for at least 90 days unless the organisation replies.
- Record opt-outs immediately; never re-contact opted-out parties.
- No scraping of closed groups, private profiles or member lists.
- No paid advertising or bulk messaging without an approved budget, platform policy review and appropriate consent.
